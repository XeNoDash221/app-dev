## 🌍 Overview
**Elden Ring** is an open world **Action RPG** developed by *FromSoftware* and published by *Bandai Namco Entertainment*.  
It was **co-created by Hidetaka Miyazaki** (creator of *Dark Souls*) and **George R.R. Martin**, the author of *A Song of Ice and Fire*.

---

## ⚔️ Key Features

- **Vast Open World** — Explore the *Lands Between*, filled with dungeons, castles, and mysteries.  
- **Character Customization** — Build your hero with different weapons, spells, and abilities.  
- **Challenging Combat** — Strategic battles that reward patience and skill.  
- **Mount & Exploration** — Ride *Torrent*, your loyal steed, across breathtaking landscapes.
